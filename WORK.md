WAITS ON: soul marks, since a contributor is verified by the merge plus a soul mark (RV.2, AT.716), and on RV.3 (whether the mark is the credential); until then the allowlist stays empty and the intake closes every issue unread.

DONE WHEN: the planted instruction is caught and every reading carries its required fields. WAITS ON: R2 for real proposals; seeded ones may run before it.

DONE WHEN: one proposal travels from intake to a ruling and back. WAITS ON: R3.
- **R5, grading and Dispatch.** A judge kept apart from the reader scores verdicts against the owner's rulings as a metrics line under Recursion's contract; the reading becomes a Dispatch template once Dispatch exists and the owner ratifies it. WAITS ON: R4, and on Dispatch.

- **R2, the allowlist.** The merge-event workflow that writes account ids to `catalog/allowlist.json`, and the open-proposal limit (RV.4, open). DONE WHEN: an account with no merge is refused and one with a merge is admitted. WAITS ON: soul marks, since a contributor is verified by the merge plus a soul mark (RV.2, AT.716), and on RV.3 (whether the mark is the credential); until then the allowlist stays empty and the intake closes every issue unread.
- **R3, the extractor and the reader, by hand.** The fixed schema and the two-stage reading (a tool-less extractor that alone sees raw text; a reader that sees only the schema and the atlas at a pinned commit), run on seeded proposals that include a planted instruction. DONE WHEN: the planted instruction is caught and every reading carries its required fields. WAITS ON: R2 for real proposals; seeded ones may run before it.
- **R4, the sign-off loop.** The queue in this house (RV.8, AT.718), one batched notice to the atlas per batch on a session branch, the owner's ruling written back to the catalog and the contributor, and the contest path. DONE WHEN: one proposal travels from intake to a ruling and back. WAITS ON: R3.
- **R5, grading and Dispatch.** A judge kept apart from the reader scores verdicts against the owner's rulings as a metrics line under Recursion's contract; the reading becomes a Dispatch template once Dispatch exists and the owner ratifies it. WAITS ON: R4, and on Dispatch.
